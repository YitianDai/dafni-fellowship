# Climate-Driven Cascading Failure Risk in Electricity Transmission Networks

**DAFNI Fellowship 2026–2027 · Dr Yitian Dai, The University of Manchester**

Website: **https://yitiandai.github.io/dafni-fellowship/**

This Fellowship brings weather-driven vulnerability assessment and sequential cascading failure simulation onto the [DAFNI](https://www.dafni.ac.uk/who-we-are/dafni-fellowship-programme/) platform, so that resilience evidence for the GB transmission system can be reproduced, examined and reused. It couples windstorm hazard fields with an open GB transmission network model and protection logic, maps where climate-driven outage risk concentrates, and shares the resulting workflows with network operators, planners and other DAFNI users. A stakeholder workshop, co-organised with the [Supergen Energy Networks Hub](https://supergenen.org/), will be held in March 2027 alongside the Hub assembly; details and outputs are on the website.

Partners: [DAFNI](https://www.dafni.ac.uk/) · [UK Research and Innovation](https://www.ukri.org/) · [The University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/) · [Supergen Energy Networks Hub](https://supergenen.org/)
